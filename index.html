New daily routine

Routines show up on staff checklists every single day and untick themselves automatically each morning. Drag between staff to reassign; tap ✎ to edit.

Completed work collects here automatically — a task from a past day moves in the moment it's ticked; today's tasks move in after midnight. Routines arrive after their day ends. Active lists stay clean while the full record keeps growing.

Daily record

The daily record keeps growing day by day — tasks are counted under the day they were assigned, routines under the day they were ticked. Deleting a task removes it from the record, so keep completed tasks if you want the full KPI history. Penalty points count every task not completed within its deadline window (30 minutes during working hours; by 12pm otherwise) — finishing late keeps the point.
